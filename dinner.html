Bacari

Bacari has many locations around LA, but when you walk in it feels like you’re in your own private universe. The environment is beautiful, but the food is even better. You can come at all times of the day and get delicious food.

Musso and Frank Grill

There are a lot of great dinner places in LA, but one of the most iconic has to be Musso and Frank Grill. The servers are lovely and the food is delicious. You are also guaranteed to run into a celeb eating here. Come eat here and eat delicious food and make lasting memories.

Saffy's

Saffy’s is a great place to eat dinner. They have delicious Middle-Eastern inspired dishes and their restaurant is beautiful. Come for their great food and cozy vibes.
